About your assistant data

The assistant uses shopping preferences such as budget, colour and size to find products. Conversation state expires after 45 minutes of inactivity. Use ?Start over? to reset it.

When enabled, Groq or OpenAI receives your shopping message, current shopping preferences and names/categories of up to five displayed products to interpret your request. Order tracking uses a separate provider and is not sent to the shopping model. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information in chat.

The assistant cart is saved in this browser until you remove items or clear this site's browser storage. It is separate from the Kiaasa website cart; checkout, prices and stock are confirmed on the website.

Location access is optional. The store finder uses your location to calculate nearby stores. Directions and map providers have their own privacy terms.

Contact Kiaasa customer support for order assistance and privacy requests. See the official website for applicable company policies.
